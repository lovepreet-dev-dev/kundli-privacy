Kundli AI Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to the Kundli AI Android app (com.kundliai.app)

The short version

Kundli AI needs your birth details to make your kundli and answer your questions. Your profile, chart and chat history are saved on your phone. Our server uses your details only to answer each request and does not keep them. We have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data.

What we use, and why

Name, birth date, time and placeYou give these to Tara, the in-app astrologer. They are sent to our server to calculate your chart and, with your chart, to our AI provider to write your readings and replies. Our server does not store them; they stay saved on your phone.
Chat messagesYour questions, plus recent messages for context, are sent to our server and our AI provider to generate Tara's reply. Our server does not store them; your chat history is saved on your phone.
Place searchThe city name you type is sent to our server, which looks it up with the Photon geocoding service (photon.komoot.io) to find its coordinates.
Install IDA random ID created when you install the app. Our server stores it with your daily count of free questions and your subscription status, so limits and Premium work without an account.
SubscriptionPayments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or UPI details. Our server receives the purchase token from Google Play to verify your subscription, and stores it with its expiry date.
Reported repliesIf you report one of Tara's replies, its text and your install ID are stored so we can review it and improve the service.
NotificationsThe daily horoscope reminder is scheduled on your phone. We don't use push servers.

Who processes your data

Chart calculations run on our own server. AI replies are generated through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which passes your request to an AI model provider to produce the reply. Place lookups use Photon by komoot. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. All connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your profile, chart, chat history and reports are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Choosing Create a new kundli in the chat menu deletes them, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything from your phone.

On our server we keep only your install ID, question counts, subscription status and any replies you reported. Subscription records are kept while your subscription is active and as required for billing records. To have the server records for your install deleted, email us (include your Google Play order ID if you subscribed) and we will delete them.

Astrology and AI

Readings are generated by AI from Vedic astrology calculations and are for guidance and entertainment. They are not professional medical, legal or financial advice.

Children

Kundli AI is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
